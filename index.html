Evidence-based supplements
Creatine Monohydrate
3–5 g daily
Improves high-intensity power output. Essential for sled push and wall balls.
Beta-Alanine
3.2–6.4 g daily
Buffers lactic acid. Helps maintain station performance late in the race.
Caffeine
3–6 mg/kg, 45 min pre-race
Proven aerobic and strength enhancer. Don't try new doses on race day.
Whey Protein
25–30 g post-session
Accelerates muscle repair. Critical for Masters athletes with slower recovery.
Magnesium
300–400 mg nightly
Supports sleep quality and muscle relaxation — often depleted in heavy training.
Vitamin D3
1000–2000 IU daily
Supports bone density, immune function, and muscle performance.
